# Knowledge articles (create in Lightning Knowledge — all fictional)

For each: **Setup → Knowledge Settings → enable**, make sure you have the Knowledge User checkbox on your user, then create the article, **Publish**, set Channel = Internal App (+ Public/Customer if needed), and add it to the agent's **Data Library / Knowledge grounding**.
Title, URL Name, Summary and Body below are copy-paste ready. The wording is deliberately specific so the agent has facts to quote and tests can check for them.

---

## 1. Seat selection policy
**URL Name:** `seat-selection-policy`
**Summary:** Who can change seats, when, and where on the aircraft.

**Body:**
- Seat changes are free of charge on all fare classes (Basic, Standard, Flex) for bookings made directly with Ascend Air.
- A seat change must stay within the same cabin. An Economy booking can only move to another Economy seat, and a Business booking to another Business seat.
- Seat changes are available online and through the virtual assistant up to **2 hours before scheduled departure**. Within 2 hours of departure, seat changes can only be made at the airport desk.
- Business cabin: rows 1–4, seats A, C, D, F.
- Economy cabin: rows 10–35, seats A–F. Rows 10–12 are extra-legroom seats.
- Extra-legroom seats (rows 10–12) are not available on Basic fares.
- Upgrades between cabins are not handled by the virtual assistant. Please contact the airport desk or the contact centre.
- A seat that is already assigned to another passenger cannot be selected.

## 2. Fare rules
**URL Name:** `fare-rules`
**Summary:** What Basic, Standard and Flex fares include.

**Body:**

| | Basic | Standard | Flex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seat change within same cabin | Yes, standard seats only | Yes, any seat in the cabin | Yes, any seat in the cabin |
| Extra-legroom seats (rows 10–12) | Not available | Available | Available |
| Date/time changes | Not permitted | Permitted with a change fee | Permitted free of charge |
| Cancellation / refund | Non-refundable | Refundable as travel credit minus a cancellation fee | Fully refundable up to 24 hours before departure |

- Fare changes, cabin upgrades, cancellations and refunds are handled by the contact centre or the airport desk, not by the virtual assistant.
- Fare conditions are set at the time of booking and cannot be changed by the virtual assistant.

## 3. Baggage allowance
**URL Name:** `baggage-allowance`
**Summary:** Cabin and checked baggage limits by fare and cabin.

**Body:**

| Fare / cabin | Cabin baggage | Checked baggage |
|---|---|---|
| Economy – Basic | 1 piece, up to 7 kg | None included (can be purchased at the airport) |
| Economy – Standard | 1 piece, up to 7 kg | 1 piece, up to 20 kg |
| Economy – Flex | 1 piece, up to 7 kg | 1 piece, up to 30 kg |
| Business (all fares) | 2 pieces, up to 7 kg each | 2 pieces, up to 32 kg each |

- Cabin bag maximum size: 55 × 35 × 25 cm.
- Excess baggage is charged at the airport. The virtual assistant cannot quote excess baggage prices.
