## 1. Seat selection policy
**URL Name:** `seat-selection-policy`
**Summary:** How seat selection works, what it costs, and which seats have extra legroom.

**Body:**
- Seat Select is an optional paid add-on. You can book a ticket without buying a seat.
- If you don't buy a seat, you can choose any free seat that is available, or you will be assigned a seat free of charge at airport check-in.
- Seat Select fees per seat, domestic / international (INR):
  - Standard seats: 200–1,700 / 200–2,000
  - Emergency extra-legroom seats: 1,000–2,000 / 1,000–5,000
  - UpFront seats (with upgrade): 1,999–2,999 / 1,999–9,999
  - Ascend Stretch seats: complimentary with Ascend Stretch and Ascend Stretch+
- Fees for bookings in other currencies are calculated from the USD rates.
- Extra-legroom (emergency exit) seats give at least 82 cm of legroom on A320-180/186 aircraft and at least 92 cm on A321 aircraft. Row numbers depend on the aircraft type, so check the seat map for your flight.
- Lite fare: a standard seat is available at an additional charge, and preferred seat selection is not included.
- Flexi Plus fare: a standard seat is included, and an extra-legroom (XL) seat is available at up to 50% off the retail rate.
- Ascend Stretch and Ascend Stretch+ fares: advance seat selection is complimentary.
- Seat selection is subject to availability.
- Upgrades between fare types are not handled by the virtual assistant. Contact Ascend Air through the website, app or contact centre.

## 2. Fare rules
**URL Name:** `fare-rules`
**Summary:** What the Lite, Flexi Plus, Ascend Stretch and Ascend Stretch+ fares include.

**Body:**

| | Lite | Flexi Plus | Ascend Stretch | Ascend Stretch+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in baggage | None included | 15 kg, 1 piece | 30 kg, 2 pieces | 40 kg, 2 pieces |
| Cabin baggage | 7 kg, 1 bag | 7 kg, 1 bag | 12 kg, 1 bag | 12 kg, 1 bag |
| Seat selection | Standard seat at extra charge | Standard seat included; XL seat up to 50% off | Complimentary | Complimentary |
| Meal | Not included | Complimentary meal | Complimentary veg meal | Complimentary veg meal |
| Change / cancellation fees | As per Ascend Air fee table | Lower than Lite (see fee table) | Change INR 3,999 (3 hours and beyond); cancellation up to INR 6,999 | Change nil; cancellation up to INR 999 |

- Lite fare is available on direct Ascend Air-operated flights only. It is not available for infants, corporate or group bookings, connecting flights, or code-share flights.
- Lite fare bookings cannot be upgraded to another fare type on the Ascend Air website or app after booking. Upgrades to UpFront or Ascend Stretch may be bought at the airport or on board, subject to availability and fare difference.
- Changes and cancellations are subject to the fees in the Ascend Air fees and charges page. The virtual assistant cannot quote a refund or waive a fee.
- Cabin baggage and checked baggage allowances are on the baggage allowance article.

## 3. Baggage allowance
**URL Name:** `baggage-allowance`
**Summary:** Cabin and checked baggage limits by fare type.

**Body:**

| Fare | Cabin baggage | Checked baggage |
|---|---|---|
| Lite | 1 bag up to 7 kg (55 × 35 × 25 cm, or 115 cm total L+W+H) | None included; can be bought before check-in or at the airport |
| Flexi Plus | 1 bag up to 7 kg (same size limits) | 1 piece up to 15 kg |
| Ascend Stretch | 1 piece up to 12 kg | 2 pieces, up to 30 kg in total |
| Ascend Stretch+ | 1 piece up to 12 kg | 2 pieces, up to 40 kg in total |

- A single checked piece cannot weigh more than 32 kg, even with excess baggage charges (regulatory requirement).
- Additional checked pieces and weight above your allowance are charged as excess baggage. Excess baggage can be prepaid online or at the airport. Flexi Plus includes prepaid excess baggage at a 20% discount.
- The virtual assistant cannot quote excess baggage prices. Tell the traveller to check the Ascend Air fees and charges page or buy it at the airport.
- Allowances above are for domestic flights. International routes can have different allowances, so tell the traveller to check their booking.
